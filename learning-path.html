How to read this page

Ordered top to bottom. Do the top block first. Nothing below it should start until its listed condition is met — that's the whole point of a sequence.

Why the order below is the order

Three constraints do all the work here, and they're already written into this workspace — this page just makes them concrete:

  • Two active subjects, max (MISSION.md). Right now that's herdr and notchtap-tauri. A third doesn't start until one of those two finishes.
  • The capstone (portable-sessions) depends on four other subjects, not just herdr. It cannot really start until herdr is finished, rust-tauri's Module 1 is done, networking's SSH/Tailscale modules are done, and someone has decided who owns the Kimi-provider spike.
  • One dependency cuts across the "two active" rule: herdr's own hdr-4 is explicitly gated on rust-tauri's Module 1 (rustlings basics). herdr is active now. So Module 1 — just Module 1, not the whole rust-tauri subject — is worth pulling forward as a small exception, not waiting for notchtap-tauri to fully finish.

What "done" looks like for this page

This sequence is right if, at every point, you know exactly one next action, it's small enough for one sitting, and it's never blocked by a subject that hasn't earned its slot yet. If a step here ever feels vague, that's the bug — shrink it, don't skip it.
