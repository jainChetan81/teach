Networking: SSH · Herdr · Tailscale · VPS queued

From "I type ssh and it works" to understanding every layer — then building a toy version of the magic yourself.

Why this track exists

Remote connections are the plumbing of your workflow, and plumbing is invisible until it breaks. This track makes the invisible visible: what SSH actually does during a handshake, how herdr establishes your remote sessions, how Tailscale punches through NAT with WireGuard — capped by building a toy tunnel and running a VPS you fully understand.

Objectives

  • Use SSH fluently: keys, config, agent, port forwarding — no copy-pasting from old notes.
  • Explain herdr's connection architecture and where it sits on top of (or beside) SSH.
  • Explain WireGuard/Tailscale: key pairs, NAT traversal, relays — then build a toy tunnel.
  • Run and harden a VPS you actually use for something.
