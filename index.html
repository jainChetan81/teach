teac

A stateful learning workspace. Synced via iCloud, versioned via git, taught in small lessons — built so that finishing is the default, not the exception.

The mission

Stop collecting study plans and start completing them. Learn the tools and systems that compound into daily workflow leverage — coding agents, Rust/Tauri, networking, LLM internals — one small win at a time, from any device.

The rule that makes it work: at most two active tracks at any moment. Everything else waits in the backlog without guilt. A track only becomes active when another one ships its final verification.

How this workspace works

PathWhat lives there
tracks/*.htmlObjectives + action items per track. Check boxes as you go — progress is saved on this device.
lessons/*.htmlShort interactive lessons, one tight idea each. This is where teaching happens.
reference/*.htmlCheat sheets and glossaries — the stuff you'll actually re-open in six months.
learning-records/*.mdWhat you learned and what surprised you. Used to pick what comes next.
plans/*.pdfYour three existing study plans, preserved as source material.
MISSION.md · RESOURCES.md · NOTES.mdAgent state — how your teacher (me) keeps continuity between sessions.
Motivation design, not motivation hope. Previous plans died of self-propagation failure, so this workspace is built around it: every action item is small enough to finish in one sitting, every track ends in something you'll use daily, and the checkboxes make progress visible from any device. When you stall, the fix is a smaller next step — never a bigger plan.
