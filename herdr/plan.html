Herdr active

The agent multiplexer that keeps your sessions alive — your first study subject, and the reference implementation for the portable-sessions capstone.

Why this folder exists

herdr (ogulcancelik/herdr, Rust) is where you feel session persistence for the first time: detach, close the laptop, reattach from your phone. Understanding how it pulls that off — a background server owning real PTYs, thin clients attaching over a Unix socket or SSH — is the formula you'll later port to t3code.

Objectives

  • Use herdr end-to-end, including reattaching from your phone.
  • Explain the client-server architecture from memory: who owns the PTYs, why clients are disposable.
  • Read the docs (and later the Rust source) for how session state survives restarts.
